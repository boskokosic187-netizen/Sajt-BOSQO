03 Process

One idea. Every format.

  1. Cats ignore the toy and play with the box. If the idea doesn’t fit in one line, the billboard won’t work either.
  2. Logo, type, palette and a hero, all chosen for that one line, not for decoration.
  3. One frame that carries the idea, composed to hold at any size.
  4. Feed, story, link ad. Each element moves to where that placement needs it, inside the safe zones.
  5. What moves, and when? Animation is part of the concept, not an export setting.

  1. Summer starts with a sound: the pssst of a cold can. The whole campaign is that one sound.
  2. A fat wordmark, a summer palette, a lemon cut phase by phase and a splash that can burst out of a can.
  3. The key visual is the label itself, flat, the way the printer gets it: dieline, bleed and a strip for the glue.
