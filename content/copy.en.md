## Case study 01: Live on the Streets

- **Client:** ITS (Information Technology School) · ITHS (IT High School) · FSU (Faculty of Contemporary Arts)
- **Years:** 2024–2026
- **Role:** Concept, design, animation, delivery
- **Deliverables:** 50+ unique billboards (animated DOOH and static), monthly campaign cycles, matching social and web assets, animated ads, animated academic calendars

**Challenge**
Three schools in one group, all competing for the attention of teenagers and parents in a loud city. A driver gives a billboard a few seconds, and a new campaign was due almost every month.

**Idea**
Treat every billboard like the first three seconds of a reel: one message, one image, one move. Then a clean end frame that still works as a static poster.

**System**
Each school gets its own colour, type and motion rules, so every new month starts from a system instead of a blank canvas. Every billboard ships with its social, web and email versions.

**Highlights**
- Pre-enrolment campaigns for the 2026/27 and 2027/28 generations
- Spring enrolment
- “The future is now”: AI school launch
- 38th Belgrade Trophy chess championship
- Graduation ceremony
- Pupin Week
- National partnerships

**Result**
50+ billboards live across Belgrade in two years, plus animated ads and animated academic calendars for both schools.

## Process: “One idea. Every format.”

**Intro**
Most of my work lives in many places at once: a feed, a story, an inbox, a billboard. Here is the whole route for one made-up client: from a one-line brief to four formats that move.

**Demo** (plays on its own while on screen; each step can be clicked)
Fictional client **purr.**, an online pet shop for cats. Meta ads in Feed 1:1, Feed 4:5, Story 9:16 and Link 1.91:1.
- Brief: new customers, −20% on the first order; cat owners 25–40 on Instagram and Facebook.
- Insight: cats ignore the toy and play with the box.
- Line: **The box is on us.** Offer: −20% on toys, food & treats. First order. CTA: Shop now.

1. **Brief → one line.** Cats ignore the toy and play with the box. If the idea doesn't fit in one line, the billboard won't work either.
2. **Elements.** Logo, type, palette and a hero, all chosen for that one line, not for decoration.
3. **Key visual.** One frame that carries the idea, composed to hold at any size.
4. **Every format.** Feed, story, link ad. Each element moves to where that placement needs it, inside the safe zones.
5. **Motion.** What moves, and when? Animation is part of the concept, not an export setting.
