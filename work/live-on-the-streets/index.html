01 The brief

Three schools in one group, all chasing the attention of teenagers and parents in a loud city. A driver gives a billboard three seconds, and a new campaign was due almost every month.

02 The idea

Treat every billboard like the first three seconds of a reel: one message, one image, one move. Make the end frame strong enough to print, and ship every board with its social, web and email set.

03Try it · the three-second read

Drive past it

Most people meet a billboard at speed, at night, for a few seconds. Pick a speed and a school, drive by, then see what a driver takes home.

      Why it mattersDesign for the glance, not the desk. A message that survives 130 km/h survives a feed.

      04The craft · 3 skills, shown

      01Motion design

      One message, one image, one move

      The text never moves, so it can be read at any frame. Everything around it does: a scanner beat, a glitch on one word, a squeegee of colour. Fifteen seconds that loop clean on an LED screen.

      02Campaign systems

      Every board ships as a set

      Logo, headline, intakes and contacts recomposed for every channel the same week: feed, email, website card and strip. One idea, every size, no weak link.

      03Beyond the billboard

      Calendars, ads and events

      The same motion language runs the academic calendar, vertical ads and event boards, so a chess trophy and a graduation feel like the same schools as the enrolment drive.

      05Result

      50+

      billboards live across Belgrade in two years

      Plus animated ads and academic calendars for all three schools: one motion language across every street and screen.
