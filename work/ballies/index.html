01 The brief

A sports prediction app needed a face. Not one mascot, a whole roster: characters fans could own, trade and play with, strong enough to front every launch for years.

02 The idea

Draw one player, perfectly. Lock the pose, proportions and line on a single rig, and let personality live in traits: headwear, eyes, arms, balls and kits. One body, 9,999 characters.

03Try it · the character system

Mix a Ballie

Every Ballie is ten layers drawn on one rig, so any trait fits any other. Spin for a new one, tap a part to swap it, lock what you like.

Layers10 layers · 99 traits

3,171,168,000 possible Ballies · 19 originals

Why it mattersA character system buys variety that never breaks the brand: billions of collectible faces that still read as one team.

04The craft · 3 skills, shown

01Character design

One rig, a roster of personalities

Pose, proportions and line weight never change, so every trait swaps cleanly and the set reads as one team. Personality lives in the details: a wink, a jester hat, a robot arm, a planet for a ball.

02Campaign illustration

The cast goes to work

The characters front everything: holiday drops, tournaments, AI features, the roadmap. A cast people recognise turned a daily posting schedule into one ongoing story.

03Key art

Sport, shot like cinema

For legends and match days: athletes mid-air, stadium light and one bold line of type. Built to stop the thumb in a feed full of odds.

05Result

9,999

characters from one rig

Then three years of seasons, launches and key art starring them: hundreds of assets in one recognisable universe.
