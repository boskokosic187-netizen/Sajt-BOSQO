Baby Boo

Baby Boo · natural cosmetics, made by moms
Year
2026
Role
Concept, art direction, generation, character animation, edit, finishing

01 The brief

Premium films and stills for a small natural-cosmetics brand. No studio, no crew, no shoot day, and the jar has to look exactly like the one on the shelf.

02 The idea

Direct it like a commercial shoot: the label stays true to the real product, the honey pours on cue, the bees sit in shallow focus. And the friends drawn on the labels get a life of their own: one ink line, cut into parts, so every one of them can move.

03Try it · the friends from the labels

Wake the cast

Every jar has a friend on its label, drawn by hand in one ink line and cut into parts so it can move. Tap one to wake it, or run bath time for all five.

☞Tap a friend

Five friends16 parts, one line

Why it mattersA mascot that moves works everywhere: a sticker, a loading screen, the next film. Drawn once, it keeps paying back.

04The craft · 3 skills, shown

01Art direction

Product-true, frame by frame

Label, mascot, cap and proportions stay true to the real jar in every frame, so the stills can sell the product, not just a mood.

02Concept stills

One bold idea per still

Break the glass in case of a sudden voyage. A claw machine full of jars. A carousel of giraffes. Each launch got one idea strong enough to post on its own.

03Product film

Cut like a commercial

Vertical first, timed to the beat, graded as one spot: the pilot runs as a 9:16 for Reels and TikTok with a 4K master for the web.
