01 Challenge

Education marketing runs on volume: dozens of programmes, careers and deadlines, each needing its own post — without the feed turning into a template graveyard.

02 Idea

Give every series a strong, cinematic signature — portrait, light, type — and let the system carry the repetition, so post 33 looks as considered as post 1.
