Series at Scale

Four series, 270+ assets, one system — careers, study programmes, AI prompts and enrolment.

Client
ITS — Information Technology School · ITHS — IT High School
Year
2026
Role
Art direction, design system, production
Deliverables
  • “What does a … do?” — 33 career profiles
  • 10 study programmes × 6 formats
  • ChatGPT Commands — 9 carousels, 117 slides
  • Enrolment 2027/28 for three schools
  • Student app launch posts

01 Challenge

Education marketing runs on volume: dozens of programmes, careers and deadlines, each needing its own post — without the feed turning into a template graveyard.

02 Idea

Give every series a strong, cinematic signature — portrait, light, type — and let the system carry the repetition, so post 33 looks as considered as post 1.

03The work

04 System

Locked grids, type scales and colour rules per series, with copy and photography swapped in through a production pipeline. Every post ships in every format its channel needs.
