01 Challenge

People judge an art faculty by its own posters. Every exhibition, lecture and enrolment deadline needed an identity good enough to hang in its own gallery.

02 Idea

Every show gets its own concept. The constant is the frame: FSU's typographic system, so twenty different worlds still read as one school.
