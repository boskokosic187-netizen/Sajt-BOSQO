01 The brief

People judge an art faculty by its own posters. Every exhibition, lecture and enrolment deadline needed an identity good enough to hang in its own gallery.

02 The idea

Every show gets its own concept. The constant is the frame: FSU's typographic system, so twenty different worlds still read as one school.

03Try it · a year of shows

Walk the gallery

So we hung them in one. Drag to walk past a year of FSU exhibitions, talks and nights, each with an identity of its own.

04The craft · 3 skills, shown

01Campaigns

One door, three stories

FSU Doors: “Create it. Move it. What did you open?” Three posts that work alone and read as one campaign in the grid.

02Programme series

One template, thirty courses

Costume Design MA: a key visual and a post per course. The frame stays, the costume and the colour change, so thirty posts never feel like one post thirty times.

03Print & presentation

From A5 flyers to lecture halls

The enrolment flyer, the study-programme presentation and the social set come from one source, so the school looks the same in your hand and on the big screen.
